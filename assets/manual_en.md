# Unit Converter — user guide

## Converter
1. Choose a **category**: length, area, pressure, temperature and more.
2. Type a number in **Value**. A dot or a comma both work as the decimal separator.
3. Choose the **From** and **To** units. The result appears instantly.
4. **⇅ Swap** exchanges the two units.
5. The table below shows the value in **every unit** of the category.
6. **Precision** sets the number of decimal places.
7. **Copy result** puts the result on the clipboard. **Save to history** adds it to the History page.

Local units: sotix (100 m²), hectare, kgf/cm², tf/m², slope in percent (%).

## Rebar table
Enter the diameter, the length of one bar and the number of bars. The app calculates the total mass in kg and tonnes.
Formula: m = π·d²/4 · 7850 kg/m³ · L. The reference table lists the cross-section and the mass of 1 m for Ø6–Ø40.

## History
A list of saved calculations. **Export to CSV** writes it to a file that opens in Excel.

## Settings
Choose Uzbek, Russian or English under **Settings → Language**, and a light, dark or system theme under **Theme**.
The app works offline.
